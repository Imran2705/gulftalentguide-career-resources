## Overview

A practical guide for Indian and international candidates applying for HSE, EHS, Safety Officer and Site Safety Officer roles across GCC industries.

## Key points

- Choose vacancies that match your actual sector experience: construction, oil and gas, manufacturing, logistics, utilities or facilities management.
- Build an evidence-led CV describing hazards managed, safety systems used, work activities supervised and responsibilities actually held.
- Read qualification requirements carefully; do not assume one certificate guarantees selection or is mandatory for every role.
- Apply through the employer's official careers channel or a verified recruitment route.
- Before accepting, verify the employer, written offer, work-authorisation process and contract terms.
