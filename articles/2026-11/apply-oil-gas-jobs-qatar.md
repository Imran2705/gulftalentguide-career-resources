## Overview

A guide for Indian applicants navigating Qatar's operator, contractor, drilling, maintenance, inspection and marine hiring routes.

## Key points

- Identify the actual hiring organisation and work environment—operator, EPC contractor, drilling or well-service provider, maintenance contractor, inspection company or marine employer.
- Match your CV to the exact discipline, asset, equipment, project phase and certificates requested.
- Search official employer portals such as QatarEnergy, North Oil Company, QatarEnergy LNG and Nakilat; verify every vacancy on the employer-controlled page.
- Check agency licensing and applicable Indian recruiting-agent registration; do not pay an intermediary for a guaranteed job or visa.
- Review the written offer, work-visa route and Indian ECR/POE requirements before travel.
