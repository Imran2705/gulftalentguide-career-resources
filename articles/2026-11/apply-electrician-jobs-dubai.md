## Overview

A practical application guide for Indian electricians targeting building-services, MEP, facilities-maintenance, industrial or panel-wiring roles in Dubai.

## Key points

- Identify the exact electrician role; building-services, MEP, industrial and panel-wiring experience are not automatically interchangeable.
- Prepare a technical CV and project record showing systems handled and work personally completed.
- Use employer career pages, licensed UAE recruitment agencies or verified Indian recruiting agents; confirm the vacancy independently.
- The guide notes UAE job seekers should not pay recruitment-agency fees to obtain a job; verify current official rules and agency licensing.
- Check the written offer, work authorisation and any Indian emigration-clearance requirement before travel.
