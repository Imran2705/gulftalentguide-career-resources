## Overview

A practical guide for Indian engineers, technicians and site professionals exploring Saudi solar PV, wind and battery-storage careers.

## Key points

- Understand the project ecosystem: developer, EPC contractor, equipment supplier and operations-and-maintenance employer may be different organisations.
- Match your evidence to the technology and role—PV, wind, battery storage, electrical maintenance, mechanical systems, controls or commissioning.
- A project announcement is not proof of a live job vacancy; apply only when an identifiable employer publishes a role.
- Check eligibility conditions carefully. The article notes that one ACWA technical campaign checked on 14 November 2026 was limited to Saudi nationals and experienced non-Saudis already residing in Saudi Arabia.
- Verify the employer's official careers page, written offer and work-authorisation route.
