## Overview

A guide for Indian mechanical maintenance technicians applying across Gulf countries, with emphasis on equipment-specific evidence.

## Key points

- Match the vacancy to the equipment actually maintained: pumps, compressors, valves, static equipment, plant utilities, port assets or shutdown systems.
- Prepare an evidence sheet describing equipment, maintenance scope, troubleshooting, overhaul work and safe completion records.
- Use a focused CV and prepare for a role-specific technical or trade assessment.
- Apply through direct employer channels or verified recruitment routes.
- Check the offer, work authorisation, contract terms and destination-specific requirements before travelling.
