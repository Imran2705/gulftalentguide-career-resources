## Overview

A role-specific application guide for Indian candidates targeting Dubai hotel and hospitality positions.

## Key points

- Target a defined department—front office, housekeeping, food and beverage, kitchen or another role—instead of sending one generic CV everywhere.
- Show genuine duties, systems used, guest-service experience and measurable results only when they can be supported.
- Use hotel group career portals and verify that the individual vacancy is current, in Dubai and open to your applicant location.
- Keep the vacancy reference and application confirmation.
- Check the written offer, work-permit route and India-side emigration requirements before paying, resigning or travelling.
