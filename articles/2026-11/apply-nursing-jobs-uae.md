## Overview

A guide for nurses and assistant nurses applying from India to UAE hospitals, focused on matching the clinical role and checking employer requirements.

## Key points

- Apply for a specific clinical role and department; registered nurse and assistant nurse roles are not interchangeable.
- Prepare a clinical CV that accurately shows education, current or recent experience, specialties and responsibilities.
- Compare each vacancy with the relevant emirate regulator's current professional requirements; eligibility or registration is not automatically permission to practise.
- Use hospital career portals and confirm vacancy status, location, closing date and employer criteria.
- Verify the written offer and work-authorisation process before making travel plans.
