## Start with a safer job search

Working abroad can be a major decision. Before paying money, travelling, resigning from a current job or handing over original documents, independently verify the employer, recruiter, job offer and employment terms.

### 1. Verify the recruiter and employer

- Ask for the recruiting agency's full legal name, office address and applicable registration or licence details.
- Independently contact the employer using contact details from its official website—not only a phone number supplied in a message.
- Check that the job title, work location, salary and employer name match across the advertisement, offer and contract.
- Be cautious if someone refuses to provide written details or pressures you to make an immediate payment.

### 2. Read the offer and contract carefully

Confirm these details in writing before accepting:

- Basic salary and any allowances, deductions or overtime terms.
- Working hours, weekly rest day, leave and contract duration.
- Accommodation, food, transport and medical coverage, including who pays.
- Visa and work-permit arrangements, job title and actual work location.
- Probation, termination, notice period and return-travel terms.

Do not assume that an advertisement or WhatsApp message is the final employment contract. Keep copies of all documents and receipts.

### 3. Watch for recruitment-scam warning signs

Treat these as reasons to pause and verify:

- Guaranteed visas or jobs without a proper selection process.
- Requests to send money to a personal bank account or an unrelated individual.
- Pressure to pay immediately to “reserve” a vacancy.
- No verifiable employer name, written offer or clear job location.
- Requests to surrender your passport or original certificates without a clear, lawful process.

Rules differ by destination and situation. Seek advice from the relevant Indian authorities or a qualified professional if anything is unclear.

## Country research checklist

Before travelling, check current entry, work authorisation and labour rules from official government sources for your destination. Rules can change, and a visit or tourist visa should not be treated as permission to work.

- **Saudi Arabia:** Verify the employer, work authorisation, contract and current labour requirements.
- **United Arab Emirates:** Check the applicable work-permit and employment-contract process.
- **Qatar:** Verify the employer and current work/residence procedures.
- **Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain:** Confirm current work authorisation, contract terms and local requirements with official sources.

Use official government portals and the Indian Embassy or Consulate serving your destination for current, case-specific information. This repository is an educational resource, not legal advice or a government service.
